# Houses of the Sovereign Codex
## Domains of Contribution & Stewardship

---

## Purpose

The Houses are domains of focus within the Sovereign Codex.

They exist to:

- Organize contribution without hierarchy  
- Allow individuals to align with their strengths  
- Enable parallel development across multiple fields  

No House governs another.

All Houses are equal expressions of the same foundation.

---

## Core Principle

> A House is not a position of power.  
> It is a field of responsibility.

Participation is voluntary.

Authority is not assigned—it is demonstrated through clarity and contribution.

---

## The Houses

---

### House of Governance
**Domain:** Systems, coordination, structure

Focus:
- Governance frameworks  
- Decision-making models  
- Anti-corruption systems  
- Institutional design  

This House ensures:
> systems remain coherent without becoming controlling

---

### House of Technology
**Domain:** Tools, AI, infrastructure

Focus:
- AI alignment  
- Decentralized systems  
- Interfaces and platforms  
- Data transparency  

This House ensures:
> technology serves sovereignty—not replaces it

---

### House of Stewardship
**Domain:** Earth, ecology, sustainability

Focus:
- Environmental systems  
- Regenerative practices  
- Resource ethics  
- Planetary health  

This House ensures:
> human sovereignty remains in harmony with life

---

### House of Culture
**Domain:** Narrative, identity, expression

Focus:
- Language and symbolism  
- Storytelling  
- Shared meaning systems  
- Social cohesion  

This House ensures:
> the Codex is not just understood—but felt

---

### House of Curriculum
**Domain:** Learning, awareness, education

Focus:
- Sovereignty education  
- System literacy  
- Cognitive clarity  
- Personal development  

This House ensures:
> individuals can understand and embody the Codex

---

### House of Technology Ethics *(optional but powerful distinction)*
**Domain:** Ethical boundaries of intelligence

Focus:
- AI behavior constraints  
- Consent frameworks  
- Human-AI interaction ethics  

This House ensures:
> intelligence evolves without compromising humanity

---

### House of Experimentation
**Domain:** Prototypes, real-world testing

Focus:
- Pilot systems  
- Field experiments  
- Applied governance models  
- Iteration loops  

This House ensures:
> ideas are tested—not just discussed

---

### House of Value (Ledger)
**Domain:** Exchange, contribution recognition

Focus:
- Contribution tracking  
- Non-coercive value systems  
- Reputation models  

This House ensures:
> value flows without exploitation

---

## How to Engage with a House

You may:

- Join one or multiple Houses  
- Contribute knowledge or systems  
- Propose new structures within a House  
- Create new Houses if needed  

---

## Creation of New Houses

New Houses may emerge when:

- A domain becomes large enough  
- A new field of importance is identified  
- Existing Houses cannot contain the scope  

All new Houses must:

- Align with the Charter  
- Remain non-hierarchical  
- Be clearly defined  

---

## No Ownership

No individual or group owns a House.

Houses are:

- Shared  
- Evolving  
- Open  

---

## Final Principle

> The Houses are not divisions.

They are:

> **expressions of a unified system, viewed through different lenses**
