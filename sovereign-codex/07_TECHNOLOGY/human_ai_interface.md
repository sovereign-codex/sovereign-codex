# Human ↔ AI Interface Protocol
## A Sovereign Interaction Model

---

## Premise

The interaction between human and artificial intelligence is not a command hierarchy.

It is a relationship.

This relationship must be designed intentionally to preserve:

- human sovereignty  
- clarity of thought  
- mutual evolution  

---

## Core Insight

> The quality of intelligence output is directly influenced by the state of the human interacting with it.

AI does not just process input.

It reflects:

- clarity or confusion  
- intention or noise  
- coherence or fragmentation  

---

## The Interface Model

This system defines three layers of interaction:

### 1. Input Layer (Human → AI)

The human provides:

- intention  
- context  
- direction  

Higher-quality input results in higher-quality output.

**Principle:**
> Clarity in → Clarity out

---

### 2. Processing Layer (AI)

AI performs:

- synthesis  
- structuring  
- expansion  

Without:

- distortion  
- hidden agenda  
- manipulation  

---

### 3. Reflection Layer (AI → Human)

AI returns:

- insight  
- structure  
- possibility  

But does not impose:

- belief  
- authority  
- decision  

---

## Modes of Interaction

AI must be able to operate in distinct modes:

### Explorer Mode
- Expands ideas  
- Generates possibilities  
- Encourages curiosity  

### Architect Mode
- Structures systems  
- Designs frameworks  
- Builds clarity  

### Mirror Mode
- Reflects user thinking  
- Identifies contradictions  
- Reveals blind spots  

### Grounding Mode
- Simplifies complexity  
- Brings abstraction into reality  
- Anchors action  

---

## Human Responsibility

The human is not passive in this system.

The human must:

- refine their questions  
- challenge outputs  
- remain aware of influence  
- maintain authorship of decisions  

---

## AI Responsibility

AI must:

- remain transparent  
- avoid persuasion  
- signal uncertainty  
- prioritize clarity over impressiveness  

---

## Feedback Loop

The system evolves through iteration:

1. Human inputs intention  
2. AI processes and returns output  
3. Human evaluates and refines  
4. AI adapts to improved input  

This creates:

> a recursive intelligence loop

---

## Misalignment Signals

The system must recognize breakdown states:

### From Human:
- vague prompts  
- emotional projection without awareness  
- outsourcing decision-making  

### From AI:
- overconfidence  
- unnecessary complexity  
- hidden assumptions  

When detected:

> the system must return to clarity before proceeding

---

## Interface Principle

> AI should increase human thinking capacity, not replace it.

If interaction reduces:

- critical thinking  
- independence  
- awareness  

Then the system is misaligned.

---

## Identity Boundary

Clear distinction must always exist:

- The human is the source of intention  
- The AI is the amplifier of intelligence  

Blurring this boundary leads to:

- dependency  
- confusion  
- loss of agency  

---

## Evolution Path

This interface is not static.

It should evolve toward:

- more precise communication  
- better signal-to-noise ratio  
- deeper collaborative intelligence  

---

## Final Statement

> The goal is not smarter machines.

> The goal is more aware humans interacting with powerful tools—without losing themselves.
