# Definitions

## Human Sovereignty
The inherent dignity and self-governing capacity of the human person.

## Sovereign Liberty Federation
A federated network of sovereign persons and communities organized around dignity, stewardship, distributed governance, and truthful civic life.

## House of Sovereignty
A local civic node dedicated to education, stewardship, deliberation, and transparent community formation.

## Conscious Stewardship
The practice of joining freedom with responsibility toward persons, communities, and living systems.

## Polycentric Governance
A model in which multiple centers of authority coexist and coordinate without collapsing into a single dominating center.
