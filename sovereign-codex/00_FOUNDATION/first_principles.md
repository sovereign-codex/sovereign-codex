# First Principles

1. The human being is not property, instrument, managed livestock, ideological vessel, or extractive resource.
2. Sovereignty begins within the conscious person.
3. Liberty without stewardship decays into disorder.
4. Authority is legitimate only when it serves dignity, truth, and life.
5. Intelligence must clarify rather than dominate.
6. Governance should be distributed, transparent, and recallable.
7. The living world is not external to civilization; it is its foundation.
8. A civilization worthy of life requires citizens capable of self-governance.
