# Amendment Process

## Rule
The constitutional core may evolve, but never casually.

## Minimum Process
1. File proposal in `12_CONTRIBUTIONS/`
2. State rationale, risks, and compatibility with first principles
3. Public review period
4. Recorded decision
5. Archive superseded version in `09_ARCHIVE/early_versions/`
6. Log amendment in `11_LEDGER/amendments/`

## Guardrail
No amendment may nullify the inherent dignity of the human person or convert stewardship into domination.
