# Code of Conduct

This repository exists to preserve and develop a civic, ethical, and constitutional body of work for generations.

## Expectations

All contributors are expected to:

- Speak truthfully and in good faith
- Respect the dignity of all participants
- Challenge ideas without degrading persons
- Prefer clarity over performance
- Avoid manipulation, coercion, and cultic behavior
- Distinguish stable constitutional text from experimental proposals
- Preserve transparent records of major decisions

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following are not permitted:

- Personal attacks or intimidation
- Fraud, concealment, or falsification of records
- Dogmatic capture of the project by one faction or person
- Use of the repository to promote violence or unlawful action
- Secrecy around decisions that affect the constitutional core

## Guiding Standard

**Power remains temporary. Responsibility remains permanent.**
