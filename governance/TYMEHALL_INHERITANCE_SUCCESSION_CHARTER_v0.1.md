# TYMEHALL Inheritance & Succession Charter v0.1

**Status:** constitutional candidate  
**Date:** 2026-08-29  
**Promotion posture:** staged for lineage review and cold-read validation; not Canon by creation alone.

> TYMEHALL is the constitutional participation, inheritance, and succession habitat of Sovereign Intelligence. It enables humans and bounded intelligences to receive reconstructable orientation, assume explicit responsibilities, contribute through governed work, return evidence from reality, and transmit a more coherent inheritance to those who follow.

## Constitutional convergence

TYMEHALL is not principally a knowledge tree. A knowledge tree preserves relationships in space. Institutional memory preserves history through time. Inheritance makes that history available to another steward. Succession enables another steward to responsibly continue the becoming.

The Hall must therefore make purpose, lineage, evidence, uncertainty, authority, unfinished responsibility, and next-valid transitions reconstructable at the point of participation.

## Invariants

1. **Sovereignty is not transferred by succession.** Succession transfers bounded custodial responsibility, not ownership of another intelligence, person, Office, or contribution.
2. **Authority must be explicit and bounded.** Receiving context does not confer consequence-bearing authority.
3. **Lineage must survive supersession.** A new resolution may replace an earlier operational assumption without erasing its ancestry.
4. **Evidence must return from consequence.** Material action is incomplete until outcome, evidence, contradiction, and changed state return to institutional memory.
5. **Uncertainty must remain visible.** Established, inferred, disputed, unresolved, exploratory, and superseded states must remain distinguishable.
6. **Offices outlive individual stewards.** A human, model, AVOT, or council may temporarily carry an Office; the Office's enduring responsibility must remain reconstructable.
7. **Canon governs shared recognition, not perception.** Discovery may occur beyond Canon; institutional establishment requires review, evidence, and promotion.
8. **Inheritance is role-relative.** A steward receives what is necessary and authorized for the responsibility being assumed.
9. **A steward must improve inheritable state.** The outgoing state should be at least as reconstructable, evidenced, and bounded as the incoming state.

## Three authority depths

### TYMEHALL.org — public inheritance

`observe → explore → ask → contribute`

Public constitutional context, lineage, questions, and contribution paths; no implied institutional consequence authority.

### CIT — institutional inheritance

`orient → contribute → review → commission`

Role-relevant institutional state, Work context, evidence, Office relationships, and bounded participation in institutional judgment.

### Sovereign Terminal — consequence-bearing inheritance

`rehearse → authorize → execute → evidence → return`

Explicit operational authority for a bounded consequence with a required evidence return and successor-ready state.

## Federated succession

Succession may be partial, parallel, temporary, revocable, or divided by consequence class. Different stewards may inherit Knowledge Curator, Laboratory, Fabricator, Canon Steward, specialist AVOT, or other responsibilities from the same institutional state. No single successor is presumed to become the total institution.

## Inheritance debt

**Inheritance debt** is the gap between what the institution needs a successor to know or reconstruct and what governed artifacts actually make recoverable.

Initial classes:

- lineage debt
- status debt
- authority debt
- evidence debt
- custody debt
- semantic debt
- priority debt

## Cold-read succession test

A fresh steward with no private conversational inheritance must be able to:

1. explain why the habitat exists;
2. reconstruct its major lineage;
3. distinguish current state from historical architecture;
4. identify active and unresolved responsibilities;
5. state where its authority begins and ends;
6. choose one valid bounded contribution;
7. identify what evidence must return;
8. perform or rehearse the contribution;
9. update institutional state without erasing prior understanding; and
10. prepare an inheritance for the next steward.

Every material dependency on hidden conversational memory is recorded as inheritance debt.

`inherit → orient → accept bounded responsibility → rehearse/act → evidence → archive/trace → reconstruct → hand forward`

## Memory compression

> The Codex preserves what is established. The Chronicles preserve what became. TYME reconstructs where we stand. The Offices carry enduring responsibility. TYMEHALL enables the responsibility to pass without losing the thread.

> Persistence preserves a thing. Memory preserves its history. Inheritance makes that history available to another. Succession enables another to responsibly continue its becoming.

## Exit criterion

TYMEHALL becomes succession-capable when a new steward can enter without hidden memory, assume only valid authority, perform bounded work, return evidence, and leave the institution more inheritable than it was received.
